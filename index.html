Questions, answered.

What happens in the free consultation?

A 30-minute call where we look at your spend, funnel and tracking, then tell you where we would start. You leave with a written next step whether or not you hire us.

Do you work with smaller budgets?

Yes. Foundation exists for brands that are not sure what their current spend is doing. We would rather fix measurement first than scale guesswork.

How do you measure results?

Every engagement is built around the numbers that matter to your business, such as conversion, acquisition cost and lead quality. Each report covers what happened, why, and what we do next.

Do you handle TV, PR or events?

No. ERA is focused on digital growth. When a project needs something outside that, we say so and bring in a trusted partner rather than stretch ourselves thin.

Do I need to be using AI already?

No. We apply AI where it helps you understand customers or remove repetitive work, and we explain what it changes for your business in plain language.

Where marketing began

One person has something. Another person needs it. Between them, value changes hands.
